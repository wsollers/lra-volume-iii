\begin{theorem}[Supremum of a Sum Set]
\label{thm:supremum-sum-set}
Let $A,B\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ be nonempty and bounded above.  Then
$A+B:=\{a+b:a\in A,\ b\in B\}$ is nonempty and bounded above, and
\[
  \sup(A+B)=\sup A+\sup B.
\]
