\begin{corollary*}[Bounded Set Has Both a Supremum and an Infimum]
Let $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ be nonempty and bounded. Then there exist unique
$s,i\in\mathbb{R}$ such that $s=\sup A$ and $i=\inf A$.
\end{corollary*}
